## Existing claims reopened in full text

- **Physically consistent 4D reconstruction, arXiv 2605.21032v1:** abstract, reconstruction/observability discussion, Table 1, ablations and Limitations. Orthogonal projected gradients address space-time credit assignment. Removing TV can slightly improve open-loop metrics while worsening novel-view extrapolation. OPG depends on clean geometry; floaters may absorb dynamics, and non-rigid pedestrians are outside the method. This is not a collision solver.
- **Real2Sim, arXiv 2605.13591v1:** abstract, pipeline figures, Section IV-E and Table II. Gaussian particles receive material parameters and evolve through MPM. The vehicle collision illustration uses 0.5 and 2.5 m/s; the authors intentionally soften material parameters to exaggerate deformation and state that unavailable true vehicle properties prevent exact realism. The review must retain this limit next to the physics claim.
- **Instant NuRec, arXiv 2607.14203v1:** abstract, training data, closed-loop experiment and Limitations. The policy-order result concerns five policy configurations on 140 scenes, with 20-second rollouts, 500 ms replanning and six trials. Its reference is the paper's internal optimized NuRec, not a paired real-world safety outcome. Thin geometry, camera-rig adaptation and coarse temporal actor interpolation remain limitations.
- **RoadVGGT, arXiv 2607.23758v1:** abstract, Section 3.3, evaluation protocol and Table 1. Waymo-trained zero-shot nuScenes evaluation; pose/depth inputs and fine-tuned Mask2Former; all quality metrics use common valid reconstruction regions; height is aligned by median Z offset. Keep its static-road role, but consolidate the six repeated paragraphs.
- **GaussianWorld, arXiv 2412.10373:** formal CVPR metadata, Section 3.3 and equations 10–12. Ego alignment and motion/perception updates incorporate the current image. “World” in the name does not make this an action-conditioned open-loop future generator.
- **GaussianFormer-2, arXiv 2412.04384v2:** formal CVPR metadata, Gaussian aggregation description and runtime discussion. The authors explicitly report that farthest-point sampling in initialization can make latency higher than GaussianFormer despite fewer primitives. Rendering opacity and semantic occupancy probabilities remain distinct quantities.

## Newly selected reports read for synthesis

- **Drive2Gauss, arXiv 2610.26585v1:** abstract, Section III, Section IV-C–F and conclusion, from the retrieval cache. Jointly generated RGB/depth/flow initialize and associate sparse Gaussian queries. Scene reconstruction is feed-forward, while generation remains a separate stage. 29.91 dB measures agreement with generated observations; 1.29 s is decoder runtime at the stated query budget. Neither is full pipeline real-time or real-world counterfactual validation.
- **SplatFlux, arXiv 2610.28257v1:** abstract, Sections 3.2–3.4 and Limitations, from the retrieval cache. Frame-wise Wan VAE encoding, latent decoding, static aggregation and forward/backward velocity interpolation. Its dynamic probability separates static and moving primitives; it is not occupancy confidence. The authors explicitly restrict the current system to offline reconstruction/generation, with streaming left for future work.
